摘要
摘要:
2026年8月26日，尼泊尔境内蓝塘里壤峰发生大规模高位冰岩崩，诱发碎屑流—泥石流—洪水跨境式复合型级联地质灾害链，沿程影响范围超过100 km，造成中国和尼泊尔两国重大人员伤亡、基础设施损毁。文章聚焦蓝塘里壤峰冰岩崩源区至我国吉隆口岸段，综合多期遥感影像、现场调查、无人机航测及滑震信号，系统研究了“8·26”高位冰岩崩灾害的发育特征、演化过程及成灾动力学机制。研究结果表明：此次冰岩崩主体由片麻岩类高等级变质岩组成，混合岩化作用较为明显，总体积约1.1×10
8
m
3
，其中冰体含量10%～15%；源区母岩发育于高喜马拉雅地块，受断层破碎带、岩性接触界面及多组结构面共同控制，形成了典型的断裂控制型易滑地质结构；在长期地震累积损伤与冰川退缩活动作用下，冰岩体自高程约
5200
m处发生高位失稳。冰岩体的断裂失稳过程呈明显的渐进多期破坏特征，主滑体失稳后高速下滑，以67 m/s的平均速度撞击错坚河冰蚀谷底，在高速高能撞击作用下迅速粉碎化并转化为高速冰岩碎屑流；当整体失稳约100 s后，运动物质进入以高速颗粒运动为主导的高度流态化阶段；在摩擦生热、冰体融化、沿程河水汇入及饱水松散物质持续侵蚀夹带的共同作用下，灾害体进一步演化为泥石流，并沿普热普藏布—东林藏布高速远距离运移，同时伴随强烈的侧向侵蚀与基底下切，两河段累计侵蚀量约5.86×10
7
m
3
，最终以22 m/s的平均流速抵达并冲击吉隆口岸。研究进一步指出，喜马拉雅高寒山区亟需构建涵盖风险源精细识别、动态评估与滑震秒级递进预警的高位远程灾害链防控体系，并提出吉隆口岸“原地后靠、抬高重建”的极端风险适应性重建思路。
Abstract:
On 26 August 2026, a large high-altitude rock–ice avalanche occurred on the northern slope of Langtang Lirung, Nepal, triggering a complex transboundary cascade of debris avalanches, debris flows, and floods. The cascade affected more than 100 km along its propagation path, causing heavy casualties and extensive infrastructure damage in both China and Nepal. Focusing on the section from the avalanche source to Gyirong Port, China, this study integrates multi-temporal remote sensing imagery, field investigations, UAV photogrammetry, and landslide-generated seismic signals to systematically investigate the characteristics, evolution, and dynamic mechanisms of the event. The results show that the main avalanche mass consisted predominantly of high-grade gneissic metamorphic rocks exhibiting pronounced migmatization, with a total volume of approximately 1.1 × 10
8
m
3
and an ice content of approximately 10%~15%. The source bedrock belongs to the Greater Himalayan block, where fault damage zones, lithological contacts, and multiple sets of discontinuities jointly formed a characteristic fault-controlled geological configuration conducive to sliding. Under the combined effects of long-term cumulative seismic damage and glacier retreat, the rock–ice mass failed at an elevation of 5 200 m. The failure process exhibited pronounced progressive and multistage characteristics. Following failure, the main mass accelerated downslope and struck the floor of the glacial valley at approximately 67 m/s in average. The high-energy impact rapidly pulverized the mass and transformed it into a high-speed rock–ice debris avalanche. Approximately 100 s after the onset of overall failure, the moving mass had fully transitioned into a highly fluidized phase dominated by rapid granular motion. Through the combined effects of frictional heating, ice melting, river-water inflow, and continuous erosion and entrainment of saturated unconsolidated material, the moving mass subsequently evolved into a debris flow and travelled rapidly over a long distance along the Purepu Zangbo–Donglin Zangbo reach. Intense lateral erosion and channel-bed incision occurred along these two river sections, with a cumulative erosion volume of approximately 5.86 × 10
7
m
3
. The debris flow ultimately reached and struck Gyirong Port at a mean flow velocity of approximately 22 m/s. The study further highlights the urgent need for an integrated framework for preventing and mitigating high-altitude, long-runout cascading hazards in the Himalayan alpine region, encompassing detailed identification of hazard sources, dynamic assessment, and progressive early warning at second-scale resolution using landslide-generated seismic signals; it also proposes a reconstruction approach for Gyirong Port that addresses extreme hazard risks by moving the port back from the river and rebuilding at higher elevations within the existing locality.
